Questions

I don't use Claude yet. What does it cost?

Claude Pro is $20 a month (cheaper yearly) and you can cancel anytime. It includes the Claude desktop app and Cowork, which the skill runs in. If you already pay for Claude Pro or higher, there is nothing else to buy.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay $27 once and keep the playbook, the skill and the bonus pack. The only subscription is your own Claude plan.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You talk to Claude in normal sentences. The code is inside the skill and Claude runs it for you.

Which Claude plan do I need?

A paid plan that includes Cowork in the Claude desktop app (Pro or higher). Claude Code works too. The free plan is not enough.

How long does an edit take?

It depends on your computer and the length of the video. As a rough guide, a 20-minute 1080p recording takes 15-30 minutes from start to finished file, and you don't have to watch.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No special hardware. Transcription and rendering run on your own Mac or Windows computer, so an older laptop is slower - it still works, you just wait longer. Phones and iPads are not supported.

Will it work for my type of video?

It is built for people talking: to camera, over a screen recording, or in a course lesson. It is not made for cinematic edits, music videos or multi-camera shoots.

Does it work in other languages?

The speech model understands dozens of languages. The playbook and the skill are written in English, and English gets the most testing.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

The skill is designed to work on your files in the folder you connect and saves every result next to the original. It never changes or deletes your original recording.

Is this made by Anthropic?

No. The AI Video Editing Playbook is an independent product that works with Claude. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic.

What if I don't like the result?

Tell Claude what to change in plain words ("keep the story at 05:20", "captions smaller") and it re-renders only that part. If it still isn't for you, email us within 14 days of purchase for a full refund - see the refund terms.
